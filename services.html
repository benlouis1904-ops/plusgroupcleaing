Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No — our cleaners arrive with professional-grade equipment and products. If you'd prefer we use your own supplies (for sensitivities or specific surfaces), just leave a note at booking.

What if I'm not happy with the clean?

Let us know within 24 hours and we'll send a cleaner back to put it right at no extra charge. That's our re-clean guarantee on every visit.

Do I have to be home?

Not at all. Many customers leave access instructions or a lockbox code. On subscriptions, your regular cleaner gets to know your preferred arrangement.

How is the final price decided?

The prices here are starting points. As you book, the estimate adjusts for your home size and any add-ons. For larger or unusually soiled homes we confirm the final figure before the clean begins.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. There are no lock-in contracts. You can reschedule or cancel any visit free of charge up to 24 hours beforehand.
