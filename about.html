About PlusGroup

We started because good help was hard to find.

PlusGroup was built to make trustworthy home cleaning in Singapore simple, reliable and fairly priced — for the people who clean and the people who book.

Our story

From a single flat to thousands of clean homes

In 2021, our founders kept hearing the same thing from friends across Singapore: finding a cleaner you could rely on — someone who showed up, did a thorough job, and treated your home with care — felt like luck, not a service.

So we built the company we wished existed. We started with a handful of carefully chosen cleaners, a written checklist, and one promise: if it isn't right, we make it right. Word spread one spotless flat at a time.

Today +65Cleaning serves homes across the island — still on the same promise, just with a lot more practice. We treat cleaning as a profession, pay our team fairly, and stand behind every visit.

What we stand for

Three things we never compromise

Trust before tasks

We hire fewer than 1 in 9 applicants, run background checks and meet every cleaner in person. You're letting someone into your home — that's a responsibility we take seriously.

A standard, not a vibe

Every clean follows our 60-point checklist, so "clean" means the same thing whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth — and whoever turns up.

Fair on both sides

Honest pricing for customers, fair pay and respect for cleaners. Happy cleaners do better work — and they stay, so you keep the same person.
